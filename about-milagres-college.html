Milagres College

Hampankatta, Mangaluru

Milagres College, Hampankatta, is a minority institution in the heart of Mangaluru, offering undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across commerce, science, and the humanities.

Alongside academics, the college has built a strong culture of sport and student initiative — inter-collegiate tournaments, athletics meets, and student-run events that give undergraduates real organising experience.

Apex Run 2026 is hosted on the college campus, with the start and finish line at the college gate. Students form the backbone of the organising team, from registration desks to media coverage on race day.
