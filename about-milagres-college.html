Milagres College

Mangaluru  •  Established 1848

Milagres College, Mangaluru, established in 1848, is one of the oldest and most reputed institutions of higher education in the region. Managed by the Catholic Board of Education and affiliated to Mangalore University, the college has built a long legacy of academic excellence spanning over a century and a half.

The college's approach centers on holistic development and values-based learning, offering students a well-rounded education that extends beyond the classroom into sport, culture, and community engagement. This same spirit of community and active living is what led the college to host Apex Run 2026.

As the host institution, Milagres College provides the venue, campus infrastructure, and on-ground coordination that bring Apex Run to life — welcoming runners, families, and fitness enthusiasts from across the region to its grounds.
