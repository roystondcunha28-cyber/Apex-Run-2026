Mangalore Runners Club

Running the coast since day one

Mangalore Runners Club is a community of amateur and competitive runners from across coastal Karnataka. The club meets for regular group runs, trains first-timers for their opening 5K, and supports members through half and full marathon seasons.

Members come from every background and pace group. The club's aim is simple — make running a normal, sustainable part of daily life in the city, and make the roads friendlier for the people using them.

For Apex Run 2026 the club serves as technical partner: route measurement and certification, pacing support, and on-course guidance for runners taking on the 5K and 10K distances.
