Mangalore Runners Club

From the coast of Mangalore — a running community built on care, consistency & camaraderie

Mangalore Runners Club (MRC) is a diverse community of runners of all ages and abilities, founded by a small group of running enthusiasts who wanted to bring like-minded people together around a shared passion for the sport. What began as a handful of members has since grown into a thriving community, gathering for weekly Sunday runs, coaching for novice runners, and monthly training programs.

Club members have gone on to compete in some of the country's best-known marathons, including the Tata Mumbai Marathon, the Ooty Ultra Marathon, and the Dandeli Ultra Marathon. MRC has also helped organize major running events in the region, including The Big Balipu marathon and the annual Niveus Mangalore Marathon, which draws runners from across the country to Mangala Stadium each year.

As a co-organizer of Apex Run 2026, Mangalore Runners Club brings its race-organizing experience and deep local running network — helping shape the route, pacing, and on-ground coordination that make for a well-run event.

Learn more or get in touch with Mangalore Runners Club at mangalorerunnersclub.com.
