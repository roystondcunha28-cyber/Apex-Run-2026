ICYM Mangalore

Indian Catholic Youth Movement

The Indian Catholic Youth Movement (ICYM) Mangalore is the official youth wing of the Diocese of Mangalore, bringing together young people across parishes for leadership formation, service, and community building.

ICYM units organise retreats, skill-development workshops, cultural festivals, blood donation drives, and outreach programmes that reach thousands of young people every year. The movement's focus is on forming young leaders who carry their values into their families, workplaces, and neighbourhoods.

For Apex Run 2026, ICYM Mangalore mobilises volunteers across the route — from route marshals and hydration stations to registration and crowd support on race morning.
